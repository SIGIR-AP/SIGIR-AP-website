Registration

REGISTRATION FOR SIGIR-AP 2026 IS COMING SOON!

SIGIR-AP 2026 is committed to fostering international collaboration and ensuring accessibility for participants from around the world.
Please note that at least one author of each accepted paper is required to register and present the paper at the conference. We allow one author to register and present multiple papers. Authors are not required to pay the full conference registration fee for each paper. Instead, they only need to pay the full conference registration fee for their first paper, plus an additional publication fee of USD 300 for each subsequent paper.
SIGIR-AP 2026 is a hybrid conference allowing all registrants to choose to attend the event virtually or in person. In-person presentations are more rewarding for both the presenter and the audience. We accordingly request that at least one author of each accepted paper make reasonable efforts to attend the conference in Singapore. If no author of a paper is able to attend in person, please make a remote presentation request to the Conference Chairs (gc2026@sigir-ap.org), explaining the circumstances that apply. These requests will be considered on a case-by-case basis.

REGISTRATION FEES

SIGIR-AP 2026 provides two types of registration, as listed below:
  • Full Passport: Full access to all conference events and services, including tutorials and workshops.
  • Workshop/Tutorial only: A welcome reception ticket and access to workshops/tutorial on the first day of the conference (13 Dec 2026).
SIGIR-AP 2026 provides registration discounts depending on your status and registration date, as listed below:
  • Early Bird: People who register before the early-bird deadline (10/11/2026 AoE) will receive this discount.
  • Online: Discounts apply if you plan to attend the conference virtually.
  • ACM Member: Valid ACM members who register for the conference will receive this discount. Please be sure to register using the email address associated with your ACM membership account to receive the member discount.
  • Student: Students who register for the conference will receive this discount. To get this discount, you are required to submit your scanned student ID card on the registration form.

REGISTRATION PORTAL

The registration portal will be available soon. Please note that:
  • ACM Membership: ACM members, please ensure that you register using the email address associated with your ACM membership account to receive the member discount.
  • VISA Support Letter: You can obtain an invitation letter for your visa application from the conference organizers by contacting sigirap2026@easychair.org, providing the following information: your passport number, name as it appears on your passport, gender, date of birth, institution, the IDs and titles of your accepted paper.
  • Registration Confirmation: You will receive a confirmation email upon successful payment.
  • Invoice: An electronic invoice for the registration fee will be emailed to you within 5 business days after the event.

CANCELLATION POLICY

No refunds except in the case of visa denial. If your visa is denied, you will be refunded the full registration amount less a $50 processing fee.
