Hotel Reservation

Accommodation

For SIGIR-AP 2026, we recommend the following accommodation options for conference participants. These hotels were selected based on their proximity to the conference venue, accessibility, and convenience for international visitors.

1. Nanyang Executive Centre (NEC)

Best for convenience / conference venue

Nanyang Executive Centre is located within the NTU campus and is also the venue of SIGIR-AP 2026. Staying at NEC provides the greatest convenience, with conference activities just a short walk away.

Why stay here

  • Located directly at the conference venue
  • No daily commute required
  • Ideal for keynote speakers, invited guests, conference chairs, and participants who prefer maximum convenience
  • Quiet campus environment

Address:
60 Nanyang View, Singapore 639673

2. Four Points by Sheraton Singapore, Jurong

Best off-campus option near NTU

Four Points by Sheraton Singapore, Jurong, formerly known as Genting Hotel Jurong, is located in Jurong East and offers convenient access to NTU.

The hotel is close to Jurong East MRT Station and major shopping malls, including JEM, Westgate, and IMM, providing a wide range of dining and shopping options.

Why stay here

  • Relatively close to NTU
  • Convenient access to Jurong East MRT
  • Wide range of nearby restaurants, shopping malls, and amenities
  • Suitable for participants who prefer to stay off campus while remaining close to the conference venue

Address:
2 Town Hall Link, Singapore 608516

3. Park Avenue Rochester

Balance between NTU and the city

Park Avenue Rochester is located near Buona Vista MRT Station and provides a good balance between convenient access to NTU and easy travel to central Singapore.

This option may be particularly suitable for participants who would like to explore the city while still maintaining reasonable access to the conference venue.

Why stay here

  • Close to Buona Vista MRT Station
  • Convenient access to central Singapore
  • Good balance between conference accessibility and city exploration
  • Nearby dining and lifestyle options at Rochester Park and The Star Vista

Address:
31 Rochester Drive, Singapore 138637
